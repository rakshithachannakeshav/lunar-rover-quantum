## What this does and does not show

- Durr-Hoyer's quadratic speedup is over brute-force search. It does not beat Held-Karp or good heuristics, and no wall-clock claim is made.
- QAOA is simulated with the tour costs applied as a lookup table, a simulation shortcut; real hardware would need those costs compiled into gates.
- Stress sets are selected for heuristic failure and are not typical instances.
- Synthetic families use made-up geometry and relief; terrain families use the simulation heightmap.
