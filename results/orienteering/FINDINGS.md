## What this does and does not show

- The exact DP finds the optimum on every instance, fast. No heuristic or quantum method beats it, and none is claimed to.
- Any QAOA gain reported above is against uniformly random sampling or a specific heuristic, never against the exact solver.
- The subset-cost table is built classically and applied directly; real hardware would need that cost compiled into gates.
- Rewards, windows and budgets are synthetic.
